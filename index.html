Where kek comes from

In World of Warcraft, the two factions don't speak the same language. When a Horde player laughs, the Alliance doesn't see "lol". The game swaps it for a word from the Orcish list. That word is kek.

[Grommash] says: lol
what the Alliance player sees:
[Grommash] says: kek
and the other way round:
[Anduin] says: lol  → the Horde sees "bur"
BEFORE WOW
